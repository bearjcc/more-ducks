# Concept — satire spine and tone

When this doc disagrees with economy numbers, see [`economy.md`](economy.md). When it disagrees with UI layout, see [`DESIGN.md`](../DESIGN.md).

## Premise

**More Ducks!** is the incremental sequel to [QLM](https://github.com/bearjcc/QLM) (Quack Language Model): a mobile-shaped chat app that slowly reveals itself as a Cookie Clicker–style satire of scaling "duck intelligence" — confident nonsense at industrial scale.

## Player journey (intended)

1. Silly chat app.
2. Cute quacks and Answerdex.
3. Addictive loop (hold Auto, feed gate, first upgrades).
4. "Flood Australia" — **more ducks**.
5. "Wait, am I the villain?" — rent, opex, idle supply.
6. Ending lands without a lecture.

## Satire rules

- **Mechanical, not preachy.** Jokes live in numbers, unlock order, and blurbs — not fourth-wall lectures.
- **Never name real AI products or executives.**
- **Ducks are earnest.** Corporate voice belongs in upgrade/marketing copy and Quackmarks, **not** in duck chat replies during normal play.
- **No IAP, no dark patterns.** Fun first; walk away happy.

## Collections

- **Answerdex:** unique quack strings (QLM table fidelity).
- **Duckdex:** breeds with mechanical "demands" on the market — collection chases scale satire.

## Endings as thesis statements

Each ending is a different industry punchline: insolvency, AGI self-governance, planetary swarm, orbital escape, or losing the market to another bird. No separate Hitchhiker "Ultimate Question" minigame — `space` and singularity copy carry those references.

## Lineage

Chat UX and quack table descend from QLM. Mechanics echo Universal Paperclips, Egg Inc, AdVenture Capitalist, Tap Titans 2 — **not** generic AI-dashboard templates.
