# Game design notes (lightweight GDD)

Pillars and phases for agents and humans. Detailed economy: [`economy.md`](economy.md). Voice: [`concept.md`](concept.md).

## Pillars

1. **Thumb-first chat fantasy** — looks like an LLM app, plays like an idle game.
2. **Numbers feel good** — taps, purchases, era shifts reward motion and sound.
3. **Pond tells the story** — sky gradient and acts replace exposition dumps.
4. **Kind satire** — cozy silly addictive, not cynical.

## Phases

| Phase | Player experience | Systems |
|-------|-------------------|---------|
| 0 — Solitary quack | Type or Auto; manual feed | Chat, coherence, Answerdex seeds |
| 1 — Bread ledger | Hold Auto; first upgrades; Auto Feeder | Upgrades tab, feed opex |
| 2 — Flock | Producers, hype campaigns | Flock + Ads tabs, market gauge |
| 3 — Boom | Capstones teased, regions | Research upgrades, Duckdex pressure |
| 4 — Hall of mirrors | Meta questions in Auto pool | Regions, Australia beat |
| 5 — Infinite wetland | Capstone purchase or orbital deploy | Endings |

## Humor style

- Upgrade blurbs: glib corporate escalation.
- Duck answers: quacks + wrong fluent trivia (QLM pools).
- Era modals: one beat of moral ambiguity, then a button that keeps scaling.

## UI north star

Tap Titans 2 bottom sheet + ChatGPT/Gemini HUD layering — see [`DESIGN.md`](../DESIGN.md).

## Out of scope

- Multiplayer, accounts, cloud saves, paid currency.
- Naming real-world AI labs or models.
